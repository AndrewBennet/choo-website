Privacy policy

Your trains, not your data.

Choo has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and doesn’t collect personal data. To show trains, the app sends our server places on the map, including roughly where you are if you’ve allowed it, and the server doesn’t keep them.

Last updated 19 November 2026. Covers the Choo app (with its widgets and Live Activities), the Choo server and this website.

The short version

  • No accounts. There’s nothing to sign up for, and Choo never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads or tracking. No advertising or analytics code, and no advertising identifier.
  • Your location only finds trains. If you allow it, the app sends our server roughly where you are while the map or Trains Here is open. The server doesn’t record it.
  • Your spots stay on your device. Where they are goes with the requests for their trains. The server keeps a spot only while you follow it on your Lock Screen or have alerts on for it.
  • Reports only if you choose. Issue reports are sent when you tap Send; crash reports only if you share analytics with app developers.

Who we are

Choo is made by Andrew Bennet, an independent developer in the UK. Email hello@choo.express to get in touch, including to ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

On your device

  • Your location, if you allow it: to show where you are, find spots near you and keep a followed Live Activity up to date. Turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services; Choo still works.
  • Your spots: their names, places and how they’re set up, shared only with Choo’s widgets.
  • The last two hours of what the server sent and what the app showed (up to about 20 MB), so a problem can be looked into. It only leaves your device in an issue report.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What the app sends, and to whom

Requests carry no name or identifier for you or your device, except the push tokens for Live Activities and alerts. The Choo server doesn’t log them, or write the places asked about to disk.

IP addresses

Every request carries your IP address. The Choo server uses it only to limit how many requests one address can make: it’s held in memory, never logged. While you follow a spot or have alerts on, the server keeps a salted hash of it with them, and deletes it with them. Our hosting provider handles requests only to deliver them. TfL, Wikipedia and Apple see your IP address under their own policies.

Issue reports

Nothing is sent until you write a note and tap Send. With no signal, it waits on your device until Choo next opens. A report contains:

  • your note, and the spot or train it’s about, if you pick one;
  • a screenshot, unless you choose “None”;
  • the app’s last two hours, which can include places on the map and where Trains Here looked;
  • your spots and the app’s settings;
  • your device’s model, iOS version, language, region and time zone.

It doesn’t include your name or contact details unless you type them in, so please don’t. Reports are read only to fix problems, may be looked over by an AI assistant, aren’t shared with anyone else, and are deleted after 60 days. To have one deleted sooner, email us with roughly when you sent it.

Crash and performance reports

If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), iOS gives Choo a technical report when it crashes, freezes or uses unusual battery or storage, and Choo sends it to our server. It says where in the code the problem was, with the app and iOS versions, device model and region: nothing about you. Reports are kept for at most 60 days and read only to fix problems.

Children

Choo is made for grown-ups to use with children, not for children on their own. It doesn’t collect personal data from anyone.

Emails from Choo

If you sign up on this website, your email address goes to Buttondown, which sends our emails (its privacy policy). You’ll get a confirmation email first. We only email you about Choo, never share or sell your address, and you can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to delete it.

This website

No cookies and no tracking. Plausible counts visits without cookies or storing your IP address: the page, the site that sent you, and your rough country, browser and device type, plus sign-ups and links followed off the site (its data policy). Like any web host, ours sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page.
