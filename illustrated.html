How it works

Find a spot. Count down together. Wave!

Most train apps tell you when a train leaves the station. Choo! tells you when it passes the place you're standing.

  1. 1

    Pick your spot

    Long-press the map beside any railway. Choo! finds the line and the stations either side, and shows what's due before you save it.

  2. 2

    Count down together

    Every spot's pin counts down to the next train, with an arrow for the way it's going, so you know which way to look.

  3. 3

    Wave!

    "Now!", then "Just gone", and the next countdown begins. Tap "A train just passed" and Choo! fine-tunes that spot's timings.

To the second

Not when it leaves the station. When it passes you.

Choo! works out when each train will pass your spot, part-way between stations. In the last couple of minutes the time comes straight from the railway's signalling, so as a train gets close the countdown is right to the second.

Spot the rare ones

The special ones get a star.

Freight trains, test trains, charters, leaf-clearing trains and engines out on their own: the ones that make a small person's day. Choo! gives each an emoji and a star on the map, and can send you a heads-up 5 minutes before one passes one of your spots.

Trains that don't stop are counted too, not just the ones calling at the station.

Stay behind the barriers.

Watch from public places behind a fence or barrier, never on or near the tracks, and hold little hands near the railway. At a level crossing, wait well back until the barriers are up and the lights have stopped.
